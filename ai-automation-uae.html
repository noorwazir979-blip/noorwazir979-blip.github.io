Questions

What is AI automation for a business?

It is software that takes over a repeated office job, such as chasing a quote or typing a supplier invoice into your accounting system, and leaves a person to check the result.

Do you only work in Abu Dhabi?

No. We serve businesses across the UAE, including Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman and the other emirates. Most of the work is done remotely.

Do we have to change our software?

No. We build around the tools you already use, such as Gmail, Outlook, WhatsApp Business, Excel, Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Tally and Odoo.

How much does it cost?

Each AI assistant (WhatsApp, website chat, email, or Instagram and Facebook) is AED 499 a month, with a 3-month minimum, then month to month. Everything else gets one fixed price after Noor understands the problem (free 20-minute check). You pay nothing until you agree.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. The assistant replies in English and Arabic, and understands Urdu and Hindi, in the language your customer writes in.

Who checks the work?

Your team. Anything involving money, a legal filing or a promise to a customer goes to a person first.
