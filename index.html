    Questions owners ask

    Straight answers

    Will it say something wrong to my customers?

    It answers only from the information you give it: prices, services, hours. If it isn't sure, it tells the customer your team will reply and sends the question to you. You test it before it goes live.

    Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

    Usually not. I connect it to your existing WhatsApp Business number. If a second number suits you better, I explain why before anything changes.

    Which languages?

    English, Arabic, Urdu and Hindi. It replies in the language the customer writes in.

    How long until it's live?

    Usually a few days: one short call to learn your business, I build it, you test it, it goes live.

    What happens to my customers' data?

    Conversations stay in your own accounts. Your information is used only to run your assistant. Read the privacy policy.

    What if I want to stop?

    Stop any month. And in the first 7 days, you get the setup fee back if it hasn't helped.
