Questions

Frequently asked

Will it say something wrong to my customers?

It only answers from the information you give it (prices, services, hours). When it isn't sure, it says your team will reply and sends the question to you. You test it before it goes live.

Do I need to change my WhatsApp number?

Usually not. Noor connects the assistant to your existing WhatsApp Business number. If a second number suits your business better, he explains why before anything changes.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Arabic, Urdu and Hindi. It replies in the language the customer writes in.

What happens to my customers' data?

Conversations stay in your own accounts. Your business information is used only to run your assistant. See the privacy policy.

What if I want to stop?

Stop any month. No long contract. In the first 7 days you also get your setup fee back if it hasn't helped.

Can I see it with my own business first?

Yes. Book a call and Noor makes a short demo with your business name and your prices.
