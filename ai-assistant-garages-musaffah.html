    Questions

    What if the price depends on the car?

    You give us your price list with ranges. For anything not on it, the assistant collects the car details and photos and sends them to your team for a quote.

    Can it talk to customers in Urdu or Hindi?

    Yes. It replies in English, Arabic, Hindi or Urdu, whichever the customer writes in.

    Do we need new software?

    No. It works on your existing WhatsApp Business number and phone line. We set it up in about a week.

    Can it do recovery and pickup requests?

    Yes. It takes the location and car details and sends them straight to your driver or team on WhatsApp.
