How it works

You talk. Spekly does the paperwork.

Speak

Say it the way you'd tell a person: "Paid 150 for diesel", "Sold 3 batteries to the garage on credit".

Spekly understands

It works out if it's a sale, an expense or a payment, who it's for, the items and the amounts, and asks you to confirm.

It's done

The invoice is made, the ledger is updated, stock goes down and today's totals move. Send it on WhatsApp in one tap.
