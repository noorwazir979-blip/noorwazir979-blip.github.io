Questions

Will it give medical advice?

No. It answers only from the facts you approve (services, prices, insurance, timings) and hands every medical question to your staff.

Where does patient information go?

It stays in your own WhatsApp, email and calendar accounts. We follow the UAE Personal Data Protection Law and only access what we need to set it up and keep it working.

Does it work with our booking system?

We check which system you use first. If it can be connected, the assistant books straight into it. If not, it books into a shared calendar your team already sees.

Which languages?

English, Arabic, Hindi and Urdu. It replies in the language the patient writes in.
