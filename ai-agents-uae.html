Questions

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that understands a request, uses your information and carries out the next step, such as booking a slot or preparing a quote, with a person approving anything important.

Is an AI agent the same as a chatbot?

No. A chatbot replies from a fixed list of answers. An agent can also act, for example by checking a price list and drafting the quote.

Will customers know they are talking to AI?

Yes. The assistant says it is automated in its first message, and customers can reach a person.

Is our customers' data safe?

We follow the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, collect consent with a timestamp, show a privacy line in English and Arabic, and delete chats after 12 months.

Can the agent answer phone calls?

Only incoming calls, on your own du or e& line, and only when agreed with you. We do not make sales calls with AI.

Do you serve companies outside Abu Dhabi?

Yes. We serve businesses across the UAE, including Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman and the other emirates.
