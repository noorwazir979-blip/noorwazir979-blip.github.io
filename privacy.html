Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Noor AI Concierge is run by Noor Zaman in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect through this website, our services and our own apps, and how we use it. We follow the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021).

What we collect

  • Forms on this website. When you book a call, send a message or ask for Spekly early access, we receive what you type: your name, business name, phone or WhatsApp number, email, and your message. The forms are delivered to our email by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co).
  • WhatsApp, calls and email with us. If you contact us directly, we keep that conversation to reply and follow up.
  • Noor's assistant and the job box. When you type into the chat in the corner of the page, or into the "What's one job your team keeps doing by hand?" box, your words are sent to our AI providers to write the answer (Anthropic's Claude, with Cloudflare Workers AI as a backup). We save these chats without your name: no IP address is stored, and phone numbers and email addresses you type are blanked out automatically. A random code in your browser groups the messages of one visit; it is not linked to who you are. We read the saved chats only to improve our answers, and we delete them after 12 months. Please don't type payment details into the chat.
  • Free demo requests. If you leave your WhatsApp number after seeing a plan, or book through the chat, that number, your job description and the plan are sent to our email so Noor can contact you.
  • No tracking. This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How we use it

  • To answer you, book calls or visits, send quotes and follow up about our services.
  • To set up Spekly early access for businesses that ask for it.
  • We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for anything you did not ask for.

Clients' business data

When we set up an AI assistant or automation for a business, we use the business's own information (prices, services, FAQs) and connect to accounts the business owns. Customer conversations stay in the business's own accounts. We access them only to set up, test, maintain and improve that business's assistant, and only for as long as the service runs. We sign a written agreement with each client describing this.

Google and YouTube data (our "Noor HQ" app)

Our internal tool "Noor HQ" uses Google's OAuth and the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to our own YouTube channel and read that channel's basic details. It does not access anyone else's Google account or data. Tokens are stored privately on our own computer and are never shared or sold. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can review YouTube's terms at youtube.com/t/terms and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Who else handles data

We use well-known services to run our work, for example Gmail (email), FormSubmit (website forms), WhatsApp and Meta (messaging), and AI providers that power assistants (Anthropic and Cloudflare for the chat on this website). Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to 24 months, then deleted, unless you become a client. Saved website chats (without names) are deleted after 12 months. Client data is deleted or returned when the service ends, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you. Email noor@nooraiconcierge.com or WhatsApp 058 935 8857. We reply within 30 days.
