    Questions

    Does it connect to Bayut, Property Finder or Dubizzle?

    Portal enquiries usually arrive on your WhatsApp or email. The assistant answers them there, so it works with the portals you already use.

    How does it know our listings?

    We load your current listings and keep them updated from the sheet or system you already use. It only shares what you approve.

    Can each agent get their own leads?

    Yes. Leads can go to agents by area, property type or a simple rotation.

    Which languages?

    English, Arabic, Hindi and Urdu. It replies in the language the client writes in.
